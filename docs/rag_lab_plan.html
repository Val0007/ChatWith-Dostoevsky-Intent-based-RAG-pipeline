What we are actually proving

Not “our RAG is better.” The claim is conditional, and that is what makes it research.

Semantic retrieval is likely sufficient for straightforward factual questions. The sophisticated machinery — intent extraction, stance metadata, evidence judging, narrative diversification — should pay off most on interpretive, motivational, comparative, and character-development questions. The deliverable is a map of which method earns its cost for which kind of question, plus a failure taxonomy that explains why.

Two deliverables, one engine

The Lab qualitative

An interactive page: one question, seven method buttons, side-by-side answers and evidence. For intuition and demos — you feel the difference. Anecdotal by design.

The Study quantitative

Gold-annotated questions, fixed metrics, N runs with confidence intervals. This produces the defensible numbers behind the finding.

Both call the same configurable retrieval engine — the Lab renders it, the Study scores it.

Eval-first

Harness + gold pilot

  • Pilot 30 questions across the 7 types; pressure-test the scheme before scaling.
  • Annotate gold evidence per question — LLM-proposed, human-adjudicated (never circular); ≥2 annotators on a subset, report κ.

Risks that decide validity

Three confounds can hollow out the finding. Each has a control built into the plan.

Confound 1
The model already knows White Nights
A famous public-domain text — the LLM can answer correctly with zero retrieval, hollowing out “answer correctness.” Control: a closed-book row 0, lean on faithfulness & citation accuracy, consider a less-canonical text as a robustness check.
Confound 2
Gold evidence is subjective
For interpretive questions the “right” evidence is contestable. Control: ≥2 annotators with reported agreement (κ); LLM may propose, humans adjudicate — never the pipeline's own model grading itself.
Confound 3
The LLM judge is a moving part
Gains at ⑤ might be “a capable model is now in the loop,” and the judge is nondeterministic. Control: hold the answering model fixed across all rows; fix seeds/temperature; N runs with confidence intervals.
