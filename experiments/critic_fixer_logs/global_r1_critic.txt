PATTERN: Overuse of "undermines" for Imaginative or Reflective Passages

AFFECTED: 8 chunks

FAILURE MECHANISM: The model is incorrectly labeling passages as "undermines" when they involve the Dreamer's imagination or reflective mood, even when these passages do not clearly depict self-deception or escapism as a substitution for reality.

PROMPT LANGUAGE: "Reserve 'undermines' for passages where the SUBSTITUTION itself is the point: the passage stages an imagined relationship AS IF it were real... or shows the Dreamer explicitly retreating from real people into fantasy."

WHY THIS CAUSES THE FAILURE: The prompt's emphasis on substitution and retreat into fantasy may lead the model to over-apply "undermines" to any passage involving imagination or introspection, even when these elements are not presented as self-deceptive or escapist.

PROPOSED FIX: Clarify that "undermines" should only be used when the passage explicitly critiques the Dreamer's imagination as a substitute for reality, not merely when imagination or reflection is present.

DRAFT INSTRUCTION: "Use 'undermines' only when the passage explicitly critiques the Dreamer's imagination or introspection as a substitute for reality. Do not apply 'undermines' merely because the passage involves imagination or reflection; these elements must be shown as self-deceptive or escapist in a way that substitutes for real connection."

PATTERN: Misinterpretation of Emotional Complexity as "Complicates"

AFFECTED: 5 chunks

FAILURE MECHANISM: The model is incorrectly labeling passages as "complicates" when they involve emotional complexity or introspection, even when the passage does not clearly honor a real feeling while questioning it.

PROMPT LANGUAGE: "complicates - the passage honors a real feeling AND the book quietly questions it in the same breath."

WHY THIS CAUSES THE FAILURE: The prompt's definition of "complicates" may lead the model to apply this label to any passage with emotional complexity, even when the passage does not meet the criteria of honoring and questioning a real feeling simultaneously.

PROPOSED FIX: Emphasize that "complicates" should only be used when both honoring and questioning a real feeling are explicitly present in the passage.

DRAFT INSTRUCTION: "Apply 'complicates' only when the passage explicitly honors a real feeling and simultaneously questions it. Emotional complexity alone does not warrant 'complicates'; both elements must be clearly present."

CLASSIFICATION: RESIDUAL MODEL ERROR — no clear prompt-level cause
